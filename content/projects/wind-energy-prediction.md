Bachelor thesis on **Gaussian Processes** and **Deep Gaussian Processes** for wind energy production forecasting. Experiments at Sotavento, a wind farm in northern Spain — where output swings with wind speed and plant constraints, so better forecasts matter for grid planning and avoiding shortages.

GPs scale to large datasets and stay non-parametric; stacking them as deep GPs adds flexibility and precision.
