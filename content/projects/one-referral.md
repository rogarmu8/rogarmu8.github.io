**OneReferral**: a one-time payment referral system built exclusively for Stripe. Turn happy customers into acquisition: lower CAC through personal recommendations, without a recurring SaaS tax on the tool itself.

# Key Features

- **Dashboard**: Manage customers, campaigns, and track progress with charts
- **Diverse Campaigns**: Limited-duration discounts, stacking caps, multi-currency
- **Stripe Integration**: Promo codes as referral inputs
- **Lifetime Access**: Pay once, single project or unlimited
