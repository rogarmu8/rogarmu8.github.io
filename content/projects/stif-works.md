**stif.works** — describe what you need; an AI agent matches you with digital professionals. Flat monthly fee for pros, no cut of their jobs.

![stif.works](cover.png)

Built and tried to validate the idea in **one week**: ideation → product → outreach. Professionals were interested, but **finding customers for them** didn’t stick — buyers wanted more context than an agent match alone. We closed it.

Still a clean loop: ship fast, talk to users, kill what doesn’t convert. Cool week.
