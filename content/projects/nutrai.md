Founded **NutrAI** as a solo build — an AI nutrition planning tool for nutritionists, fitness coaches, and health pros. Personalized meal plans in seconds, tuned to client preferences with live updates.

**Role & stack:** Founder · solo · JavaScript, Next.js, MongoDB

**Impact:** **40+ professionals** using the app after cold outreach · B2B product from zero to paying users

# Key Features

- **AI Plan Generator**: Meal plans tailored to each client in seconds
- **Extensive Meal Database**: Large meal library plus custom recipes
- **Interactive Plans**: Shareable plans with client feedback loops
- **PDF Export**: Customizable exports in multiple languages
- **Client Management**: Contacts and communication in one place
- **Progress Tracking**: Adjust plans from real client results
